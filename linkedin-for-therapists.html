The problem

Having a LinkedIn profile and having a LinkedIn presence are completely different things.

Most therapists and psychologists have a LinkedIn account they set up years ago and occasionally log into. It has a profile photo, a job title, and very little else. It is a missed opportunity of significant scale.

LinkedIn is where HR managers, corporate wellness leads, EAP coordinators and other professionals who refer clients actually spend time. It is also where media look for expert sources and where other practitioners find referral partners.

But showing up on LinkedIn with authority requires confident, opinionated, professionally assertive content. We write it in your voice, so you never have to be someone you are not.

"On LinkedIn, you are not competing with other therapists for clients. You are positioning yourself so the right opportunities find you without you having to chase them."

What we do

Five things that build real LinkedIn authority.

  • Blog posts and articles

    Long-form content that builds your authority on Google and answers the questions your ideal clients are already searching for. Written in your voice, not a template.

  • Service pages

    Pages that explain what you do, who it is for, and why someone should choose you — without sounding like every other therapy website. Optimised for search and for trust.

  • Social media copy

    Posts for Instagram and LinkedIn that educate, reassure and invite — never exploit or sensationalise. Content that builds a following of the right people.

  • Email newsletters

    Regular touchpoints with your existing audience. Keeps you present and trusted between sessions and referrals.

  • Website copy

    Home page, about page, FAQ — every word considered. The difference between a visitor who leaves and one who books a call is almost always the copy.

  • Content strategy

    A 6-month editorial plan built around the searches your clients make and the topics that build your authority. So you never stare at a blank page again.
