Most therapists and psychologists in India have a website. Almost none of them appear when a potential client types "therapist near me" or "anxiety counsellor Bangalore" into Google.

You spent years building your expertise. Your website sits on page four — behind directories, behind larger clinics, behind practitioners who figured out Google before you did.

What we do

Six things that move your practice up Google — and keep it there.

  • Google Business Profile

    Set up, verified and fully optimised so you appear on Maps when someone searches for a therapist in your city. Most practices have this set up incorrectly — or not at all.

  • Keyword research for mental wellness

    We find the exact phrases your ideal clients search — city-specific, condition-specific, profession-specific. Not "therapy" — "CBT therapist for anxiety Pune."

  • On-page optimisation

    Titles, headings, content structure and page copy rewritten so Google understands exactly what you offer and who you serve.

  • Technical audit and fixes

    Page speed, mobile experience, indexing issues. The invisible problems that quietly kill rankings without anyone noticing.

  • Content strategy

    A long-term plan for building authority in the mental wellness space. Each piece of content compounds. Six months from now it is still working for you.

  • Monthly reports in plain language

    Where you rank, what changed, what is next. No jargon, no padding, no vanity metrics. Just what matters to your practice.

The build process

How a practice gets a website it is proud of.

Phase 01

Discovery

A single conversation to understand your practice, your clients, your values and what the website needs to achieve. We ask the questions most designers never think to ask.

Phase 02

Design

Visual direction and copy developed together. You see how it looks and how it reads before a single line of code is written. Revisions happen here, not after launch.

Phase 03

Build

Your approved design is built to be fast, mobile-first and SEO-ready from day one. We do not cut corners that become problems six months later.

Launch

You sign off. We go live. Then we check everything — speed, indexing, contact forms, mobile display. You start with a site that works properly from the first day.
