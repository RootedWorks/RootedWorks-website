The problem

The fear of saying the wrong thing keeps most therapists from saying anything at all.

Mental wellness is one of the most sensitive spaces on social media. One poorly worded post can feel exploitative. One clinical caption can feel cold. The fear of getting it wrong is legitimate — and it paralyses most practitioners.

The result: inconsistent posting, months of silence, the occasional post that felt safe enough, and a social presence that does not reflect the quality of the actual work.

We understand the ethical weight of this space. Every piece of content we create is reviewed through one lens: would a person in distress find this helpful, respectful, and honest? If yes, it goes out. If not, it does not.

"Your clients spend months watching your content before they send that first message. What they see in that time determines whether they reach out at all."

The first 30 days

What the first month looks like.

Week 1

Ethics and voice briefing

Before we write a single word, we spend time understanding your specific ethical considerations. What topics you will not touch, what language you find reductive, what your values are around mental health content. This is not a checkbox — it is the foundation everything else is built on.

Weeks 2 – 3

First content batch, your approval

We create the full first month of content and send it to you for review. Every post, every caption. Nothing is scheduled until you have read it, approved it, and are comfortable with it going out under your name.

End of Week 4

You go live consistently

Content begins publishing on schedule. For most practitioners this is the first time their social media has been consistent. The difference in how it feels — and how their audience responds — is usually immediate.

Month 2 onward

Steady presence, monthly rhythm

A monthly cycle begins. Content delivered, reviewed, approved, scheduled. You spend perhaps 20 minutes a month on social media. We handle the rest. Your presence stays consistent whether you are in session, on leave, or simply living your life.
